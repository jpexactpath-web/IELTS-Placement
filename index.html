IELTS Placement Test

Academic English Language Assessment

Welcome to the IELTS Placement Test. This comprehensive assessment evaluates your English language proficiency across listening, reading, and writing skills.

The test is designed to mirror the format and difficulty of the official IELTS Academic examination and will help determine your current English language level.

Test Structure

Section 1: Listening

40 questions  |  40 minutes  |  Various question types including fill-in-the-blank, multiple choice, and matching

Section 2: Reading

40 questions  |  60 minutes  |  Three passages with comprehension questions

Section 3: Writing

2 tasks  |  60 minutes  |  Task 1: 150+ words  |  Task 2: 250+ words

Before You Begin

  • Ensure you have a stable internet connection
  • Find a quiet environment free from distractions
  • Allocate approximately 2 hours and 40 minutes for the complete test
  • Have headphones ready for the listening section
  • Prepare a valid email address for submitting your results

Important Notes

Your answers will be automatically saved as you progress through each section. A countdown timer will track your time for each section. When you submit the test, a TXT of your answers will be downloaded and an email draft will open for sending to your instructor.
